# Global Renewable Energy Transition (2015–2024)

A Tableau dashboard exploring how renewable energy production, capacity and investment changed across countries and regions between 2015 and 2024.

**Project type:** University group assignment · Data Visualisation Using Tableau

![Global Renewable Energy Transition dashboard](Tableau%20Dashboard%20Final.png)

## What we explored

- Renewable energy production across countries and regions.
- Changes in renewable energy capacity over time.
- Investment patterns and their relationship with production.
- Adoption patterns across different income groups.

## Dashboard features

The dashboard brings together a global map, country leadership rankings, an income-level comparison, an investment-versus-production chart and regional trend lines. Filters allow the data to be explored by **region, year and energy source type**.

## Key observations

Our group presentation highlighted increasing renewable energy capacity from 2015–2024, particularly for solar and wind. It also noted differences across income groups and a generally positive association between investment and production. These are observations from the assignment, not evidence of causation.
